# Sphere League — Team Crest Art Direction (provisionally approved)
Date: 2026-11-19
Owner decision: Warren approved the latest six-crest concept as good enough for current testing. No further redesign now; may revisit later.

## Six teams and visual identities
- Lakewood Riptide — cyan/blue wave, gold trident.
- Mile High Currents — violet and gold, mountain/current motif.
- Boulder Breakers — burnt orange/charcoal, cracked stone and industrial frame.
- Aurora Anglers — electric/acid green with black, bioluminescent angler motif.
- Durango Divers — crimson/red and brass, vintage tech-fantasy diving helmet.
- Pikes Peak Undertow — icy white/cobalt, crystalline mountain/vortex.

These are the latest *concept* directions, not verified exact official hex colors; Claude should reconcile with game/team palette values before production integration. Aim for distinct silhouettes, distinct team-color edges, and readability at small mobile sizes. Reduce uniform cyan outlines. Maintain original Sphere League art, not copied imagery from other games.

## Production handoff
The reviewed six-crest concept board is not six separate transparent 256×256 production PNGs. Do not treat concept approval as completion of file export. Separate/clean each crest at 256×256 with transparency and padding, inspect text/lettering, and request game-scale review before integration.

## Owner note
Current style is approved for testing, but overall art may later shift further toward technology + fantasy and away from repetitive coral/underwater decoration. Avoid redesigning now.
