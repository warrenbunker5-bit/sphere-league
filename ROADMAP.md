# Sphere League roadmap

## Revisit later
- **Wake-up runs:** let the player send a teammate to wake a sleeping player. It pulls them out of position and costs stamina. Balance it against the "sleep the keeper, then score" strategy, which should stay strong but not automatic, for both attack and defense.
- **One stamina bar:** possibly fold SP into HP so moves also cost stamina, like the original.
- **Landscape mode:** try a wide layout with the sphere centered and the panel at the side.
